HARDlight has always had a strong relationship with Unity. During the development of *Sonic Dream Team*, Unity approached us about writing a blog post exploring how the game was created.

I had the opportunity to co-author the article with our Associate Engineering Director. I never imagined I’d get the chance to write something like this, but here we are!

The team at Unity Japan also enjoyed the article and published a translated version on the Unity Japan website.

You can read both versions here:

* [English](https://unity.com/resources/hardlight-sonic-dream-team)
* [Japan](https://unity.com/ja/resources/hardlight-sonic-dream-team)
